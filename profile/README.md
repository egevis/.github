## About EGEVIS

**EGEVIS Technologies** is a machine vision and industrial software engineering
company headquartered in İzmir, Türkiye. Our legal name is **EGEVIS Endüstriyel
Yazılım Teknolojileri A.Ş.**

We design and implement high-performance visual quality control systems for
manufacturing. Our work combines optics, cameras, lighting, image processing,
artificial intelligence, industrial software, controls, and mechanical
integration in one production-ready solution.

Every application begins with the manufacturing process—not with a
one-size-fits-all product. We study the part, defect criteria, cycle time,
material behavior, handling, environment, and plant interfaces before defining
the inspection architecture. The result is a system engineered around the
customer's real quality requirements.

Our goal is straightforward: make every inspection **reliable, understandable,
traceable, and useful to production**.

## Product spotlight: VIS360

### 360° inline surface inspection for continuous profiles

**VIS360** is EGEVIS's multi-camera surface inspection platform for continuous
products such as automotive sealing profiles. Its eight-camera architecture is
designed to observe the complete product circumference while the profile moves
through the production line.

VIS360 brings optical inspection, defect evaluation, production monitoring,
traceability, and line integration together in one system.

Key capabilities include:

- 360-degree visual coverage with a purpose-built multi-camera arrangement
- Continuous, inline inspection at production speed
- Custom illumination engineered around profile geometry and material behavior
- Detection and evaluation of surface and process-related anomalies
- Recipe-based configuration for different product references
- Real-time operator feedback and inspection visualization
- Production statistics, alarms, event history, and result reporting
- Defect image retention and traceability options
- Marking, reject, PLC, and upstream/downstream equipment integration
- Optional AI-assisted inspection for challenging or highly variable features
- Remote diagnostics and lifecycle support

VIS360 reflects our broader engineering philosophy: **control in every
millimeter, confidence in every product.**

- Application studies, sample evaluation, and feasibility testing

- Monochrome, color, infrared, and application-specific imaging
- Lens, field-of-view, resolution, working-distance, and depth-of-field design
- Diffuse, backlight, dark-field, directional, and custom illumination

- High-speed acquisition, synchronization, triggering, and image buffering

### Algorithms and industrial AI

- Pattern matching, edge analysis, segmentation, morphology, and metrology
- Surface, shape, color, texture, and contrast evaluation
- Rule-based inspection for deterministic and explainable decisions
- Supervised classification for known product or defect categories
- Anomaly detection for variable defects and limited defective samples
- Dataset preparation, annotation strategy, training, validation, and deployment
- Hybrid inspection pipelines that combine classical vision with machine learning
- Threshold, confidence, and severity management matched to production criteria

We select algorithms according to the application. AI is used where it adds
measurable robustness—not as a substitute for sound optics, stable acquisition,
or clear acceptance criteria.

## Our engineering principles

### Production first

A successful vision system must remain dependable under real line conditions:
changing parts, vibration, dust, reflections, operator interaction, maintenance,
and production pressure.

### Optics before complexity

Stable images are the foundation of stable decisions. We prioritize correct
lighting, optics, mechanics, triggering, and part presentation before increasing
algorithm complexity.

### Clear decisions

Operators and quality teams should understand what the system found, where it
was found, and why the result matters.

### Measurable performance

Inspection quality is validated with representative data and production
criteria, including repeatability, detection performance, and false-call
behavior.

### Maintainable systems

We build for the full equipment lifecycle with diagnostics, documentation,
configuration management, backups, training, and support.

### Responsible integration

Safety, production continuity, data handling, access control, and plant
standards are considered throughout the system design.
